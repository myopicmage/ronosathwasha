Basic sentences

Ronesathwasha in its native script, with romanization and grammatical glosses retained for reference.

Rose Latin text marks a form the current script cannot encode. The consonant h was never admitted, and its affected spatial words still await respelling. Legal syllables around them are rendered normally.
