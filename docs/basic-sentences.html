Basic sentences

Ronesathwasha in its native script, with romanization and grammatical glosses retained for reference.

I eat

 

Lari thinəme

I (subject marker) food (present tense)

More commonly



thinəme (drop subject)

I am eating

 

Lari thinəmedi

I (subject marker) food (present tense) (continuous)

More commonly



thinəmedi

I do not eat

  

Lari ma thinəme

I (subject marker) no food (present tense)

More commonly

 

ma thinəme (drop grammar markers)

The teacher teaches

 

Mirireri mirime

Teach-person (subject marker) teach (present tense)

The teacher is teaching me

  

Mirireri layi mirimedi

Teach-person (subject marker) me (object marker) teach (present tense) (continuous marker)

(You) teach me (now)

 

Layi yomirime

I (object marker) (command marker) teach (present tense)

(I) eat that food (over there)

 

Sho thinəme

That (over there) food (present tense)

I might eat that food

  

Lari sha lithinəme

I (subject marker) that (over there) (might) food (present tense)

More commonly

 /  

lithinəme or sha lithinəme

My dog eats

  

Lasa runəru thinəme

I (possessive) dog (subject marker) food (present tense)

Do you eat?

 ?

Nari tethinəme?

You (subject marker) (question marker) food (present tense)

(I) want to eat

 

Thinəswe twame

Food (infinitive) want (present tense)

More commonly

 

thinə twa (drop markers, becomes “I want food”)

(I) want that (thing)

 

Shamwuyi twame

That-thing want (present tense)

More commonly

 

shamwu twa (object and tense markers dropped, implied)

I go home

  

Lari taði medime

I (subject marker) home (towards) go (present)

More commonly

 

ta medi (drop subject, location marker, and tense marker)

(Person) has a dog

  

Ðari runəyu time

Alive-thing (subject marker) dog (object marker) have (present tense)
