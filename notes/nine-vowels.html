What this is

A sketch, not a proposal and not part of the language. It shows data/script.toml with three vowels added in the trapezoid cells that are currently empty, so that vowel harmony has three vowels per class to work with instead of two.

Nothing was drawn for it. A chevron's bearing is derived as Direction((backness, height)), which has always had nine cells and six occupants. The three new vowels use the three bearings no chevron pointed at, and the glide ticks follow because a tick is antipodal to its point.

The costs are real and only two of them are cheap. glide_offset goes from 6 to 9 and the vowel run grows from E020..E02B to E020..E031, both of which are declared. The keyboard is the awkward one: every vowel currently sits on the letter it is romanised with, and æ, å and ɨ have no ASCII home.

Built from notes/nine-vowels.script.toml. To rebuild it, load that file with ronesathwasha.script.load and pass the result to tools.webfont.compile_woff2. It carries its own font, as every page of this script must.
