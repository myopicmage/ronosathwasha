## Notes

Binder path: `Places / Ancient World / Notes`

- Capital city was thought to be in the southern half of The Rabbit, uncertain\\
- Southhaven claims that they were the site of the capital, but this remains unverified and hotly contested
- Began to fall when the northern reach was invaded
  - Modern historians have not made the connection between the northern reach and the fall of the civilization since texts from the era would not have referenced it
  - The “fall” was more of a slow decline as the rules changed more and more. A dissipation
- Something about the invasion fundamentally changed the rules of the world, causing once-stable society to slowly fall
- Roughly equal to modern society in terms of knowledge and understanding, though the rules were different. Magic from the era is inherently unstable
  - Ancient knowledge was not lost, though as the civilization continued to decline, the desire to share it diminished
  - The world became less trusting
- Civilization structured similarly to EU or star trek federation
  - Unified disparate cultures under a shared language and market
  - Membership was entirely voluntary

## Language

Binder path: `Places / Ancient World / Language`

Translates to “people’s tongue”.

Used similarly to modern common, though much more robust. Used as an everyday
language unlike modern, much more utilitarian Common.

Knowledge of the language was shared through magic books that would update as
the language was grown and shaped by a central council. The book network
failing is one of the many reasons cited for the fall of the civilization. When
the spell failed, the books went blank, leaving no definitive reference for the
language, and requiring it to be passed down via other resources. Eventually,
it fractured and split and was no longer taught as it stopped being a truly
common language.

## Details

Binder path: `Places / Ancient World / Language / Details`

- Strict consonant + vowel morphemes
- SOV word order
- Modeled loosely after korean, japanese, and finnish

## Grammar

Binder path: `Places / Ancient World / Language / Grammar`

### Basic Rules

- Verbs imply the copula in simple sentences
- SOV word order
- Most markers can be dropped depending on context
- A noun can be turned into a verb by adding any of the verb markers (ex. runeswo: “to dog”)

### Markers

- Command marker: `yo(verb)`
- Continuous tense marker: `(verb)ji`
- Infinitive: `(verb)swo`
- Might: `li(verb)`
- Negation (not/don’t): `ma (verb)`
- Plural marker: `(noun)ne`
- Possessive marker: `(noun)sa`
- Question: `tho(verb)`

### Particles

- Location (at): `-shi`
- Location (in): `-thi`
- Location (near): `-mi`
- Location (towards/in the direction of): `-hi`
- Object: `-yi`
- Subject: `-ju`

### Tense markers

- Present: `(verb)me`
- Past: `(verb)je`
- Future: `(verb)the`

### Demonstratives

- This: chi
- That: cha
- That (over there): chu
