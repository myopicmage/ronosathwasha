# Smaller things to mine

Status: research leads, not canon.

This is a holding place for isolated distinctions that may be useful to Rono
but do not yet justify a language-sized research note. An entry records the
semantic pressure worth investigating, not a proposed form or grammatical
decision.

If an entry develops several related seams or needs sustained comparison with
its source language, promote it into its own note.

## Korean `되다`

The dictionary form is `되다`; the familiar conjugated form `돼` comes from
`되어`. It is often translated as "become," but the useful pressure is broader:
a transition has occurred, and a resulting state now obtains. The construction
can foreground the result without presenting an agent as deliberately causing
it.

Distinctions to mine:

- being in a state against entering that state;
- becoming something against being deliberately made into it;
- an intended change against circumstances ending up that way;
- whether completing the transition entails that the resulting state holds.

Rono should answer those pressures using its own existing distinctions before
adding a general change-of-state mechanism.
